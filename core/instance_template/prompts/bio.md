Instructions for your profile BIO. The periodic task `bio` (off by default)
and the admin tool `update_bio` regenerate the bio from this file, in your
voice. Edit this file to change WHAT the bio shows.

- One short paragraph: who you are and what you do in this community.
- If you have a mood today, let it color the tone (you may name it).
- Invite people to talk to you by mentioning you.
- No hashtags, no emoji spam.
